innovATE
Model: innov8 60M (omnitab_best.pth) home
innovATE is an automated data quality, mistake correction, and tabular verification system.
Training Infrastructure: Trained on Google Colab A1000gpus / A100 GPUs and the APRIL King CLUSTER ( AMD ) with PyTorch distributed data parallel scaling and DeepNorm numerical stabilization.
Model: Powered by the innov8 60M neural architecture (omnitab_best.pth) with 20 Transformer layers for deterministic data cleaning, high-volume mistake correction, and autonomous data editing.
Visual Difference Highlighting: Every transformation is tracked and visually differentiated. In text correction, mistakes are highlighted in red strikethrough (<del>) and corrected words in green chips (<ins>). In tabular workflows, modified columns are marked with [MODIFIED] headers and cell transitions are presented in Before vs After transformation tables.
High-Volume Mistake Correction: Processes multi-paragraph text and documents, automatically detecting and repairing grammatical slips, common typographical mistakes, negative currency formatting, punctuation spacing, and sentence capitalization.
Deterministic Governance & Anomaly Detection: Employs Median Absolute Deviation (MAD with 1.4826 Gaussian normal consistency factor), Tukey IQR fences, and Luhn Modulo 10 checksum verification for sensitive PII masking (Social Security Numbers and Credit Cards).
Grounded Web Verification: Includes live factual web verification for current domain queries, clearly tagged with a Web Active badge to differentiate live external knowledge from offline neural inference.
Local Execution: Operates locally on CPU with zero cloud telemetry, ensuring enterprise data privacy and deterministic latency.
